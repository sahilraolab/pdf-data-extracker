Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get access to PDF Suite? +
Contact us using the form on this page or reach out on WhatsApp. We set up every account personally after a quick conversation. There's no self-registration — this ensures every supplier gets the right setup for their volume and workflow.
How much does PDF Suite cost? +
Pricing is discussed personally and tailored to your usage. We don't publish a fixed price list because every supplier's order volume and needs are different. When you contact us, we'll understand your requirements and give you a transparent price. There are no hidden charges.
Does PDF Suite work with all Meesho label PDF formats? +
Yes. We continuously update PDF Suite to stay compatible with Meesho's label PDF format. If you encounter a PDF that isn't processing correctly, contact us on WhatsApp and we'll fix it quickly — often within 24 hours.
Is my order data safe with PDF Suite? +
Yes. Uploaded PDFs are deleted from our servers after processing. We do not sell or share your order data with anyone. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Please read our full Privacy Policy for details.
Can I use PDF Suite on my phone? +
PDF Suite works best on a desktop or laptop browser for uploading and reviewing large PDFs. The website is responsive and will work on a phone or tablet for basic use, but we recommend a desktop for daily operations.
How quickly can I get started after contacting you? +
Usually within one business day. Once we've had a quick chat to understand your setup, we create your account and share login credentials. We also do a short walkthrough so you're comfortable using the tool from day one.
