Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan later? ▼
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is there a free trial for paid plans? ▼
The Professional plan comes with a 7-day free trial. No credit card required to start. Business plans can be evaluated via a demo — contact us to schedule one.
What payment methods are accepted? ▼
We accept UPI, net banking, debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and Paytm. All payments are processed securely via Razorpay.
What happens if I exceed the page limit? ▼
If your PDF exceeds the page limit for your plan, you'll be prompted to upgrade. Pages beyond the limit won't be processed on the Starter plan.
Can I get a refund? ▼
We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee for all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, contact support within 7 days of your purchase for a full refund.
Is my data safe? ▼
Uploaded PDFs are processed in memory and deleted immediately after analysis. Generated filtered PDFs are stored for 1 hour for re-download, then permanently deleted. We do not store your invoice data.
